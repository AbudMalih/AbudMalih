# Luna Trading · Phase 2: Refinement, Internationalization, Commerce

Phase 1 architecture is unchanged (chapters, stage store, globe, line-world, sequence player). This document records what Phase 2 changed.

## 1 · Hero
- `--fs-mega` reduced by about 18 % and made height-aware: `clamp(3.2rem, min(13.2vw, 23.5svh), 16rem)`.
- **Protected header zone.** `--header-h` and `--safe-top`. Every stage starts below `--safe-top`, and the hero headline is bottom-anchored inside it.
- Arabic gets its own height-aware size, because its glyphs are taller.

## 2 · Reverse wordmark (no plate)
- `public/brand/luna-trading-logo-reverse.webp` is generated by `scripts/build-reverse-logo.sh`. It is a pure per-pixel colour mapping of the master: luna → `#A6A7AC`, trading → `#F2F2F0`, "+" stays `#C90216`. Alpha and anti-aliasing are copied unchanged, so geometry is identical.
- The master (`luna-trading-logo-trim.webp`) is untouched and is still used on the paper footer.

## 3 · E-commerce as a pillar
- **Chapter 05** is now the six-step operating model: Beschaffung → Import → Entwicklung → Marke → **E-Commerce** → Distribution.
- At E-Commerce the line opens into **"Vom Produkt zur Plattform."**: product origin → platform layers → four market endpoints (marketplaces, direct-to-consumer, B2B platforms, EU market access). Data packets move with the scroll. There are no carts, bags, phones or checkout screens.
- E-Commerce also appears in the hero route index, in the warehouse labels and on the ecosystem dial.

## 4 · LUVISCENT transition
- The palette is now deep forest with olive undertones, champagne `#CDB68A`, ivory `#EFE9DC` and amber `#C8904F`. The emerald is gone.
- **Sequence:** at the end of the chain the graphite falls away and the red line warms to amber, then champagne, while an amber light leak enters. The forest and olive atmosphere rises, the line becomes the horizon, a champagne key light opens, and the mark appears last. Leaving reverses it back to Luna red.

## 5 · Em dashes
None remain in user-facing copy. Every rendered page has been checked; the count is 0 for `/`, `/en` and `/ar`.

## 6–10 · Languages
| URL | Language | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `/` | Deutsch (default) | `/de/*` → 308 to `/*` |
| `/en` | English | |
| `/ar` | العربية | `lang="ar" dir="rtl"`, IBM Plex Sans Arabic |

- `middleware.ts`: there is no Accept-Language detection. The `luna-lang` cookie is written only by the DE · EN · AR selector, and it is honoured on unprefixed URLs. An explicit choice is never overridden.
- **RTL:** the layout uses logical properties, and the chain, commerce fan, rail, scrims and route rule all mirror. Logos, globe, line-world and the brand claim do not mirror. Arabic is never letter-spaced; coordinates stay in Latin mono.
- The brand claim "Eleganz liegt in der Luft." is German in every language. The Impressum is German in every language, with a note in EN and AR.

## 14 · Content architecture
```
content/i18n/config.ts      locales, default, cookie, localePath()/stripLocale()
content/i18n/types.ts       Dictionary type (every key required in every language)
content/i18n/{de,en,ar}.ts  all copy
content/i18n/I18nProvider   useI18n() → { locale, dict, dir, href }
content/ecosystem.ts        structure only (ids), no copy
app/[lang]/…                all routes statically generated per locale
```
Adding a page means adding its keys to `types.ts` and filling them in each dictionary. TypeScript fails the build if any language is missing a key.
